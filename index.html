Why hold
The War Chest

💰 Creator fees flow back to the kingdom.

Every pump.fun trade pays the coin's creator a fee. The Aperoyal war chest pledge: a share of creator fees funds prizes for the arena — weekly drops to the top killers on the champions board and the highest lords of the Royal Court. The exact split and schedule are posted in the community channels before each round. No contracts, no lockups, no promises of profit — just the dev putting trading fees back into the game that drives them.

Straight talk
Read this before you ape
This is a memecoin.

Aperoyal is a game layered on a pump.fun token. Ranks, capes and crowns are cosmetic and social — they are not equity, yield, or a claim on anything. Never spend what you can't afford to lose.

The arena sees what it sees.

Fights and ranks are computed from the live trade feed while the page is open. The Royal Court is real on-chain holder data, refreshed about every 90 seconds from a public Solana RPC — if the RPC is busy, the court may lag.

War chest is a pledge, not a contract.

Prize drops come from creator fees at the dev's discretion, announced in community channels. There is no smart contract enforcing them.

Champions are per-browser for now.

The arena champions list is stored in your own browser. The Royal Court, however, is the same for everyone — it's read from the blockchain itself.
